Purchases

I paid, but this phone isn't showing my household

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase, just below the Puddles Pro card. This asks Apple to confirm the purchase and hands your household back. It is the right fix after a reinstall, a new phone, or a purchase that was interrupted partway.

The app says "Almost there" or "Purchase not found"

The payment and the setup are two separate steps, so it is possible for the money to go through and the household not to arrive. Tapping Restore purchase finishes the job. It is safe to tap more than once — it will never charge you twice or create a second household.

The app says it "couldn't match the purchase to a household"

This one needs us. The app deliberately stops rather than risk creating a second household and stranding your history behind the first. Email us and we'll sort it out.

Do I need to buy it twice for two phones?

No. One person buys Household Sync, then shares the household code. The second person taps Have a code? Join a household and enters it. Only one purchase is ever needed per household.

Notifications

I'm not getting notified when my partner logs something

  1. Check both phones are in the same household — the code in Settings should match exactly.
  2. Check notifications are allowed: iOS Settings → Puddles → Notifications.
  3. Check Sleep mode is off in Puddles' own settings. It pauses reminders on purpose.
  4. Fully quit Puddles and reopen it on both phones.

Notifications are only sent to other people in the household. You will never be notified about something you logged yourself.

Your data

How do I get my history out?

Settings → Export logs gives you the whole history as a CSV file you can open in any spreadsheet.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data.

On a free household this erases everything on the phone, and there is no copy anywhere else. On a synced household it also deletes the household and its whole timeline from our servers — for everyone in it, not just you. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want to keep the history.

What do you collect?

On the free tier, nothing at all — it never leaves your phone. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
